Problem statement

Attackers move silently. You need them to make noise.

Standard defenses wait for a breach. Proactive security sets traps and monitors every endpoint to catch unauthorized movement early, giving you full visibility before an attack matures.

The visibility gap

Security teams often lack visibility into internal lateral movement. Without decoy systems or continuous endpoint analysis, attackers can test boundaries and exploit vulnerabilities completely unseen.

01
Attackers test boundaries unseen. Without honeypots, internal reconnaissance and port scanning blend into background noise.
02
Endpoint vulnerabilities go unnoticed. Traditional monitoring fails to connect specific endpoint vulnerabilities to active threats.
03
Lack of real-time payload analysis. Teams need raw interaction transcripts and payload captures to understand attacker intent.
Solution statement

Deploy traps. Analyze endpoints. Stop movement.

AntiTOD unifies an Adaptive Honeypot Network (AHN) with an Endpoint Threat Analyzer (IETA) to give you complete visibility into attacker behavior and endpoint risk profiles.
